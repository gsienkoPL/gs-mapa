description=GS Mapa: Polish spatial planning register (POG, MPZP, ZPI, PZPW), OpenStreetMap and BDOT10k basemaps, and WMS/WMTS/WFS services from the national EZiUDP catalogue, plus APP planning-act files with official symbology and GML validation.
about=GS Mapa - a QGIS 4 plugin. One dock with product tabs: the Spatial Planning Register
    (general, local, integrated and voivodeship plans served as PMTiles from gs-mapa.cloud,
    plan information, act documents), OpenStreetMap and BDOT10k basemaps from the
    gs-mapa.cloud server, and a browser of the Register of Spatial Data Sets and Services
    (EZiUDP, GUGiK) that adds the reported WMS, WMTS and WFS services as layers. The APP Files
    tab opens planning-act GPKG/GML files styled with the official APP symbology, validates
    GML against APP 2.0/3.0 (XSD, specification rules, geometry) without a database, converts
    older versions to APP 3.0 and creates new acts with a wizard.

    - Description and about in English, a changelog field, the Web category and English tags.
    - APP XSD schemas shipped as unpacked files instead of the app_xsd.zip archive.

    0.1.0 - First public release
    - Plans tab: spatial planning register acts (POG, MPZP, ZPI, PZPW) with official symbology,
      national layers of the register and the plan information tool with act documents.
    - GUGiK tab: search of EZiUDP data sets, WMS/WMTS/WFS layers, WFS download to GeoPackage
      and county data packages (cadastral parcels, buildings, property price register).
    - APP Files tab: acts opened with the official APP styles, GML validation against
      APP 2.0/3.0, conversion to APP 3.0 and a wizard for new acts.
    - OpenStreetMap and BDOT10k basemaps as raster or vector layers.
    - Light and dark theme support in the dock and in the dialogs.
